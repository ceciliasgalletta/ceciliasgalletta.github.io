Welcome!

I am a theoretical and computational astrophysicist, working in the field of binary compact objects, massive star evolution, and gravitational waves across cosmic time. I earned my Ph.D. from SISSA in Trieste, Italy, with a thesis titled "Binary neutron stars and binary black holes: from the Milky Way to the high-redshift Universe". I am currently a Humboldt fellow at the Institut für Theoretische Astrophysik in Heidelberg, Germany.
